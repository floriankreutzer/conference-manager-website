## Gap classification

### Homepage — partially fulfilled

Strengths: clear conference-management positioning, buyer-led problem/value sequence, Demo-first conversion, room-booking differentiation, PAVUREL endorsement, product-family control geometry.

Gaps: visual system remains primarily CSS/editorial rather than asset-led; PAVUREL explanation is too brief to serve visitors who intentionally want company/brand context; product experience can be tied more clearly to real product value.

### Product — partially fulfilled

Strengths: customer-oriented product narrative and verified capability framing.

Gaps: review for remaining internal vocabulary and stronger product-experience context; add a clearer next step and visual support where source evidence permits.

### How it works — partially fulfilled

Strengths: simple staged journey and customer framing.

Gaps: ensure every step expresses user/operational outcome rather than workflow mechanics; visual support should communicate the journey rather than architecture.

### Integrations — not yet at target quality

Current copy still contains implementation/evaluation vocabulary including tenant connection, provider access, calendar synchronization, integration authority, provider-specific behavior and server-side integration.

Target: lead with adoption fit, reuse of existing Microsoft 365 / room-booking capabilities and controlled enterprise enablement; retain technical qualification only where an IT evaluator needs it, using customer-readable language.

### For Workplace Teams — partially fulfilled

Strengths: directly addresses Workplace Team work and real request/change capabilities.

Gaps: some lifecycle/process wording remains internally framed. Tighten around preparation, ownership, visibility and controlled change outcomes.

### Security & Trust — not yet at target quality

Current copy reads partly like architecture documentation: access/refresh tokens, authenticated session state, browser authority, API boundaries, server-side processing, request limits, endpoint acceptance, repository evidence and production-origin terminology are too prominent for a public trust page.

Target: explain separation of public website and secure product access, data minimization, controlled integrations and evidence-led security in buyer/IT language. Keep implementation mechanics in repository/technical documentation rather than customer copy.

### Pricing — partially fulfilled

Strengths: correctly fails closed and does not invent prices.

Gap: the page is primarily an explanation of why pricing is not published. Reframe around how buyers can evaluate fit now, while retaining the publication gate and no-offer rule.

### Insights index and article — largely fulfilled

The first bilingual Insight is customer-readable and educational. Review navigation, next-step links, and metadata so Insights are part of the conversion journey rather than a dead-end content area.

### Book a demo — partially fulfilled

Strengths: appropriate prospect journey and fail-closed endpoint behavior.

Gap: operational availability notes should not dominate customer copy; keep configuration limitations concise and user-facing.

### Brand / Company — not fulfilled as a destination

The current homepage/footer explain the endorsement, but there is no dedicated customer-facing place to understand PAVUREL without mixing brand explanation into product pages.

Target: add a compact Company / PAVUREL page grounded only in approved sources: Conference Manager remains the product; PAVUREL is the preferred corporate-brand candidate/endorsement; brand expression combines operational precision with warm workplace hospitality; legal clearance remains pending. No invented company history, mission, values or legal adoption claims.

## Implementation rules for this follow-up

- Marketing pages lead with customer problem, outcome and operational value.
- Technical/evaluation pages may retain technical substance, but only after customer-readable framing and only where relevant to evaluation.
- No public page should expose internal architecture terminology merely to prove implementation sophistication.
- Every major public destination ends with a useful next step.
- Company/PAVUREL context stays subordinate to the Conference Manager product story.
- No unapproved AI/master placeholder image will be presented as production-cleared photography.
- If the supplied AI visuals are explicitly approved for public website use, integrate selected visuals with responsive formats, intrinsic dimensions, alt-text strategy and existing performance budgets.
